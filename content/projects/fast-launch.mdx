The knowledge is kinda abstract and we could explore ways to offer customized experience and reduce the back-n-forth communication in the process.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cs-placeholder">Payment gateway setup</div>

<div className="cs-split cs-split--wide-left cs-block">

  <div className="flex-vertical gap-20">
    <h4>2. Self-serve device setup</h4>
    <p className="body-md">We don’t have accessible instructions for card reader setup therefore we offer the human assistance by appointment.</p>
    <p className="body-md">We could explore breaking down the most common device setup instructions into steps to enable self serve setup, while keeping the human assistance option available if the merchants need it.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div className="cs-split cs-block">
  <div className="flex-vertical gap-20">
    <h4>3. Bridge the gap between contract signing and portal registration</h4>
    <p className="body-md">In the current state, the merchants still rely heavily on the sales team for negotiating the rates and finish the contract signing through Docusign, and 57% of merchants never register for the portal afterwards. Although the number dropped to 30% after the Fast Launch release, there is still a significant experience gap between the contract signing and portal registration.</p>
    <p className="body-md">We are also working on a separate initiative to move the application process into the portal, and communicating the value of the portal early on in the E2E workflow.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="cs-placeholder">Contract signing to portal registration</div>
</div>

<div className="cs-split cs-split--wide-left cs-block">
  <div className="cs-placeholder">More intuitive device tracking</div>
  <div className="flex-vertical gap-20">

    <p className="body-md">Due to the current technical constraint, our merchants still need to go from the portal to UPS site to track their device shipment status.</p>
    <p className="body-md">To reduce the friction, in future phase, we will explore leveraging the UPS API and host all the tracking information within the portal.</p>
